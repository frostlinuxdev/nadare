# DW-Proton 11.0-17 as the pinned runner (2026-11-14)

Released upstream 2026-11-13 15:14 UTC (dawn-winery/dwproton-mirror). Pinned for b24 by the maintainer's decision.

**Integrity.** The project's published `dwproton-11.0-17-x86_64.sha512sum` verifies the download, and the computed
sha256 equals GitHub's asset digest: `dd8af140eacca2bb78ac3cdd641649091dbf65c30c24ede36cef8e875fbb0dd8`
(342,043,716 bytes).

**What it is.** Same Wine base as 11.0-16 (`wine-11.0 (CachyOS)`), newer DXVK (`v3.1.1-198`; 11.0-16 has
`v3.1.1-97`).

**Real GPU, headless (AMD Ryzen 3500U / Vega 8, RADV, `gamescope --backend headless`), 2026-11-13.** Same method
as the 11.0-16 report (game CPU and `gpu_busy_percent` over 10 s after 150 s):

| Game | 11.0-17 | 11.0-16 |
|---|---|---|
| Stella Sora | 179% CPU, GPU 69% busy | 188% CPU, GPU 70% busy |
| Arknights, default (`dxvk.enablePresentTiming = False`) | 39% CPU, GPU 23% busy | 39% CPU, GPU 23% busy |
| Arknights, workaround off | 40% CPU, GPU 23% busy | (same as default) |

**Not checked yet.** Chaos Zero Nightmare on 11.0-17 (its sign-in needs a QR scan), and the Arknights
present-timing hang, which only showed on NVIDIA with KWin (the newer DXVK may change it either way).

**Everything through umu (#1340).** Videos in NIKKE play on 11.0-14, -15 and -16 under umu and the Steam Runtime on
an RTX 3060 (2026-11-14); without the Steam Runtime they fail on any of them (`libgstlibav.so: libvpx.so.9` missing).
